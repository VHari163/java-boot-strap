YouTube

This page provides information about YouTube, showing a sample image and a brief description of the platform as a popular website for watching and sharing videos.

YouTube is a popular video-sharing platform where users can upload, view, and share videos. It offers a wide range of content, including music videos, tutorials, vlogs, and more. Users can subscribe to channels, like and comment on videos, and create their own playlists.

Launched in 2005, YouTube is the world's largest online video-sharing platform. It allows billions of users to discover, watch, upload, and share originally-created videos. It functions as both a social media network where users can interact via comments and likes, and the second-largest search engine in the world right behind Google.
